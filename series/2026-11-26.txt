--- description
Tell me about yourself for freshers | Best answer to tell me about yourself | HR interview questions
Interview prep 🎤
Resume mat padho. 3 hisse, 30 second:
1. Aaj: abhi kya ho, ek line ("final year computer science student")
2. Kal: ek kaam jo kiya, proof ke saath (project, internship, number)
3. Aage: is role se kyun judna hai
Sample: "I am a final year computer science student. I built an attendance app that forty students in my college use, which taught me backend basics. I want to grow as a backend developer, and this role lets me do that."
Hobbies tabhi, jab poochein.

Kal: saath rejections ke baad kya karein.
