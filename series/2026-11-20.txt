title: Interview prep | Explain your project in 3 lines
cast: arjun, bunty
jobs: india-freshers, 0
hook: PROJECT: SIRF 3 LINE
trial_hook: Don't list every feature of your project. Interviewers remember three lines.
trial_card: PROJECT = 3 LINES
Bunty: Project mein login, dashboard, chatbot, sab samjhaya. Interviewer ne beech mein rok diya.
Arjun: Kyunki tumne syllabus suna diya. Sab bologe toh kuch yaad nahi rahega.
Arjun: Format: problem, tumhara hissa, result. Teen line.
Bunty: Problem?
Arjun: Hostel complaints register mein likhte the, aur hafton kuch nahi hota tha.
Bunty: Mera hissa?
Arjun: Sirf jo tumne kiya. Maine backend aur database banaya. Team ka kaam apna mat bolo.
Bunty: Result?
Arjun: Ek number, agar sach hai. Kitne students ne use kiya, kitna time bacha.
Bunty: Aur chatbot?
Arjun: Wo tab, jab wo poochein. Agla sawaal tumhari teen line se hi aayega.
Bunty: Matlab main decide kar raha hoon wo kya poochenge.
Arjun: Exactly. Problem, hissa, result. Syllabus nahi.
Arjun: Follow karo, kal gap year ka sawaal.
