Interview prep 🎯
Na "hardworking", na "passionate". Sab yahi bolte hain, isliye yaad nahi rehta. 3 part ka jawab:
1. Zaroorat: job description se ek cheez
2. Proof: project, internship ya result
3. Plan: pehle mahine mein kya karoge
Example: "Aapko SQL reporting chahiye. Maine fest ka data SQL mein saaf kiya. Pehle mahine dashboards samjhungi."

Save karo. Kal: strengths aur weakness ka sahi jawab.
