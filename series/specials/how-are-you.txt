How to answer how are you in interview | Small talk in interview for freshers | HR interview tips
Interview prep 👋
Small talk ka formula: chhota jawab, phir sawaal wapas.
1. How are you? → "I'm good, thank you. How are you?"
2. How's your day going? → "Good so far, thanks for asking. How's yours?"
3. Did you find the office easily? → "Yes, it was easy, thank you."
4. How was your weekend? → "It was relaxing, thanks. How was yours?"
5. Nervous ho? → "A little, but I'm excited about this role."
Pehle hi jawab mein nervous, raat bhar padhai ya traffic ki kahani mat sunao.

Kal: tell me about yourself ka poora jawab.
