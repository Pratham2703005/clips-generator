200 applications but no interview call | Fresher job search tips | Off campus jobs India
Do sau applications, zero calls? Ye luck nahi, 3 galtiyan hain:
1. Har job pe same resume. Job description ke 5 skill words resume ke top par (sirf jo sach mein aate hain).
2. Purani job pe apply. 2 din ke andar wali job chuno.
3. Apply karke chup. Recruiter ko 2 line ka message bhejo:
"Hi, maine aaj <role> ke liye apply kiya. <skill> mein 2 project kiye hain. Ek baar resume dekh lenge?"

Tumne kitni applications bheji? Comment mein number likho.
