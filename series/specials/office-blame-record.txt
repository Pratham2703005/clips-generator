Office tips 📝
Galti kisi aur ki, blame tumhara? Aksar isliye, kyunki baat sirf call pe hui thi. Join karte hi ye 3 aadatein daal lo:
1. Call ke baad 2 line ka mail: "Jaisa baat hui, main kal tak report bhej dunga."
2. Roz shaam 3 line: kya kiya, kya atka, kal kya. Appraisal mein yahi proof hai.
3. Plan badle toh likhit mein lo: "Ek baar chat pe confirm kar dijiye."
Appraisal ya kisi jhagde ke waqt yahi mails aur notes kaam aate hain.
