Malhotra: Stop bluffing in interviews. Pata nahi bolna fail nahi hai.
Bunty (shocked): Par Ma'am, chup raha toh lagega mujhe kuch nahi aata.
Malhotra: Isliye teen step. Pehla: do second ruko. Bolo, ek second sochne dijiye.
Bunty: Itna bol sakte hain?
Malhotra: Haan. Ghabrahat mein jaldi-jaldi bolna zyada bura lagta hai.
Bunty: Doosra step?

Bunty: Aur agar bilkul zero pata ho?
Malhotra: Teesra: sach bolo, plus plan. Ye maine abhi nahi padha, main aise seekhunga.
Bunty: Aur bluff kiya toh?
Malhotra: Agla sawaal usi pe aayega. Wahin pakde jaoge.
Bunty: Matlab interviewer answer nahi, soch dekh raha hai.
Malhotra: Bilkul. Ek sawaal pe interview khatam nahi hota.
Bunty: Kal next round hai. Ruko, jo pata hai, sach plus plan.
Malhotra: Save kar lo. Kal sach bolna, no bluffing.
